Course Listings

Graduate and undergraduate courses taught at USCA.

CSCI 617 — Advanced Information and Network Security

Advanced graduate-level topics in network security protocols and implementations (IP/DNS/Email security, cloud security, intrusion detection and prevention systems, etc.).

CSCI 620 — Introduction to AI and Machine Learning

Preparation in core AI and machine learning concepts with applications to intrusion detection, malware analysis, and security analytics (theory and hands-on labs).

CSCI 591 I & II — Capstone Seminar for Cybersecurity Students

Team-based, in-depth study of a selected cybersecurity topic with faculty supervision and sustained theoretical and implementation guidance across two semesters, culminating in a complete and technically sound project.

CSCI 515 — Ethical Hacking

Preparation for the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) certification (theory and hands-on labs).

CSCI 616 — Advanced Computer Networks

Advanced graduate-level topics in networking (congestion control, Quality of Service, SDN, etc.).

CSCI 425 — Network Security

Core principles of network security, including intrusion detection, prevention, firewalls, secure protocols, traffic analysis, and risk management.

CSCI 215 — Information Security

Preparation for the CompTIA Security+ certification.

CMPS 200 — Introduction to Programming

Fundamentals of computer programming and problem solving (C, Java, Python).

CMPS 212 — Intermediate Programming with Data Structures

Intermediate and advanced programming concepts (data structures, complexity analysis, program verification).

CMPS 209 — Computers and Programming for the Sciences

Essential computer skills needed by students in science and business majors.

ITEC 445 — Advanced Computer Networks

Advanced administration of client/server networks and preparation for CCNA certification.

ITEC 591 — Data Science

Introduction to the data science lifecycle, covering data collection, cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualization, and applied statistical and machine learning methods for extracting insights from real-world datasets.
