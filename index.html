Biography

I received my Ph.D. in 2024 from the University of South Carolina (USC) focusing on P4-programmable data planes for offloading network security functionalities. My doctoral research investigated how to implement Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) at line rate, with a particular emphasis on detecting malicious attacks that exploit the Domain Name System (DNS).

Alongside my research, I developed extensive hands-on instructional materials in networking and cybersecurity, covering topics such as Border Gateway Protocol (BGP), Open Shortest Path First (OSPF), Software-Defined Networking (SDN), programmable data planes, and applied cybersecurity. The hands-on materials have been used by thousands of learners across the globe.

My current research builds on this foundation and expands into security for Industrial Control Systems (ICS), hybrid network- and host-based defense mechanisms, attacks on generative AI systems, and next-generation high-performance technologies, including Smart Network Interface Cards (SmartNICs), P4, and extended Berkeley Packet Filter (eBPF).
